EMMY-T CONCEPT – PREMIUM CORPORATE WEBSITE DEVELOPMENT BRIEF

1. Project Overview

I want to develop a premium, modern, high-end corporate website for EMMY-T CONCEPT, a professional Graphic Design, Digital Printing, Advertising and Branding company.

The website should establish EMMY-T CONCEPT as a credible, reputable and professional brand with a strong online presence.

I want a complete website with a visually impressive frontend and a fully functional backend administration system.

This should not look like an ordinary small-business website. I want the quality, structure, appearance and functionality of an established professional company website.

2. Brand Identity and Visual Direction

The website must follow the exact colour identity of my official logo.

Brand colours:

* Luxury Black
* Metallic Gold
* Premium Silver
* White for clean contrast and readability.

Use my supplied EMMY-T CONCEPT logo as the official brand logo. Do not redesign or alter it.

The overall appearance should be:

* Premium and luxurious.
* Modern and corporate.
* Elegant and visually attractive.
* Clean, organised and easy to navigate.
* Professional typography and excellent spacing.
* Smooth animations and transitions.
* High-quality images and presentation.

Avoid overcrowded layouts, cheap-looking templates and excessive animations.

3. Website Frontend – What Visitors Should See

A. Homepage

Create a powerful homepage featuring:

* Professional navigation menu.
* Prominent company logo.
* Attractive hero section with a strong headline.
* Brief company introduction.
* Explore Our Services button.
* Request a Quote button.
* Featured services.
* Selected portfolio projects.
* Why Choose Us section.
* Customer testimonials.
* Strong contact section.
* Professional footer.

B. About Us

Include:

* Company introduction.
* Our story.
* Vision and mission.
* Core values.
* Commitment to quality and customer satisfaction.

C. Our Services

Create individual service sections or pages for:

1. Graphic Design.
2. Banner, Sticker & Label Printing.
3. Flyer Printing.
4. Business Card Printing.
5. Product & Packaging Branding.
6. Clothing & Fabric Printing.

Each service should have a professional description, relevant images and a button to request a quotation.

D. Portfolio / Our Projects

Create an attractive portfolio gallery where visitors can view our completed works.

Include categories such as:

* Graphic Designs.
* Banner Designs.
* Flyer Designs.
* Business Branding.
* Packaging Designs.
* Clothing and Fabric Printing.
* Other Printing Projects.

Allow visitors to view individual projects in larger detail.

E. Request a Quote

Customers should be able to submit project enquiries through a professional form.

The form should collect:

* Customer’s name.
* Phone number.
* Email address.
* Service required.
* Project description.
* Required quantity and dimensions, where applicable.
* Upload design or reference files.
* Preferred delivery date.

After submission, the enquiry should be saved in the backend for management.

F. Contact Us

Include:

* WhatsApp contact button.
* Phone number.
* Business email.
* Social media links.
* Business location, when provided.
* Contact form.

4. Backend – Admin Dashboard

This is a very important part of the project.

I want a secure and fully functional backend dashboard that only authorised administrators can access.

The dashboard should allow me to manage the website without needing a developer every time I want to make changes.

Admin Dashboard Features

1. Dashboard Overview

* Total enquiries.
* New orders.
* Pending quotations.
* Completed projects.
* Recent customer activities.

2. Service Management

* Add new services.
* Edit existing services.
* Delete services.
* Update service descriptions and images.

3. Portfolio Management

* Upload new project images.
* Add project titles and descriptions.
* Organise projects into categories.
* Edit or remove portfolio projects.

4. Order and Enquiry Management

* View customer enquiries.
* View uploaded reference files.
* Update enquiry status.
* Manage customer requests.
* Track orders from pending to completed.
* Record quotations and agreed prices.

5. Website Content Management

* Edit homepage content.
* Update company information.
* Change promotional banners.
* Update contact details.
* Manage testimonials and announcements.

6. Customer Management

* View customer details.
* Access enquiry history.
* Keep track of customer requests.

7. Admin Account and Security

* Secure administrator login.
* Password protection.
* Password reset functionality.
* Role-based access for additional staff.
* Secure logout and session management.

5. Technical Requirements

I want the website to have:

* A proper frontend and backend architecture.
* A reliable database.
* Secure authentication and authorisation.
* Responsive design for mobile, tablet and desktop.
* Fast loading speed and optimised images.
* Search engine optimisation (SEO).
* SSL/HTTPS security.
* Automated backups.
* Error handling and form validation.
* A scalable structure for future expansion.

The website should also support WhatsApp integration so customers can easily contact the company.

6. Website Navigation

The main navigation should include:

HOME | ABOUT US | SERVICES | PORTFOLIO | REQUEST A QUOTE | CONTACT

The Admin Dashboard should be separate from the public website and protected by administrator authentication.

7. Final Expectations

I want a complete, production-ready corporate website, not just a frontend design or static landing page.

The frontend must impress visitors, communicate professionalism and convert visitors into customers.

The backend must give me full control over website content, services, portfolio, customer enquiries and order management.

The website should be built with quality, security, scalability and long-term business growth in mind.

Brand Name: EMMY-T CONCEPT

Brand Identity: Luxury Black, Metallic Gold and Silver.

Brand Positioning: Professional Creative Design, Digital Printing, Advertising and Branding Solutions.

Brand Personality: Premium, Creative, Innovative, Reliable and Professional.

Overall Goal: To establish EMMY-T CONCEPT as a strong, reputable and recognised creative company with a premium digital presence.

EMMY-T CONCEPT – COMPLETE WEBSITE & ADMINISTRATOR FUNCTIONALITY

1. ADMIN DASHBOARD

Create a powerful, modern and easy-to-use admin dashboard where I can manage the entire website and monitor business activities.

The dashboard should display:

* Total website visitors.
* Total customer enquiries.
* New orders.
* Pending orders.
* Completed orders.
* Pending quotations.
* Recent customer activities.
* Recent website updates.

Include a clean analytics section with charts and statistics to help me monitor business performance.

2. PORTFOLIO & PROJECT MANAGEMENT

This is one of the most important features.

As the administrator, I want to be able to:

* Upload pictures of newly completed projects.
* Upload multiple pictures at once.
* Upload project videos where supported.
* Add project titles and descriptions.
* Select project categories.
* Add completion dates.
* Edit or replace uploaded pictures.
* Delete projects.
* Feature selected projects on the homepage.
* Rearrange the order in which projects appear.
* Publish projects immediately or save them as drafts.

Whenever I upload a new project and publish it, it should automatically appear in the appropriate portfolio section on the public website.

Include categories such as Graphic Design, Banner Printing, Flyer Printing, Business Branding, Packaging, Clothing Printing and other projects.

3. SERVICES MANAGEMENT

I want complete control over the services displayed on the website.

I should be able to:

* Add new services.
* Edit service names and descriptions.
* Upload service images.
* Add service categories.
* Remove discontinued services.
* Rearrange service listings.
* Mark selected services as featured.
* Add or update service information without contacting a developer.

New services should automatically appear on the public website once published.

4. CUSTOMER ORDERS & ENQUIRIES

Create a proper order and enquiry management system.

Customers should be able to submit orders and project enquiries through the website.

The system should allow me to:

* View all customer enquiries.
* View customer contact information.
* View project descriptions and requirements.
* Download customer-uploaded designs and reference files.
* Record quotations and agreed prices.
* Update order status.
* Add private administrative notes to orders.
* Search and filter orders.
* View previous customer orders.

Order statuses should include:

* New Enquiry.
* Awaiting Quotation.
* Quotation Sent.
* Awaiting Payment.
* In Production.
* Ready for Collection.
* Dispatched.
* Completed.
* Cancelled.

5. CUSTOMER MANAGEMENT

Create a customer management section where I can:

* View registered customers where customer accounts are enabled.
* View customer contact details.
* View their enquiry and order history.
* Identify returning customers.
* Add internal customer notes.
* Search for customers by name, email or phone number.

6. WEBSITE CONTENT MANAGEMENT

I want to be able to update the website myself.

The admin dashboard should allow me to:

* Change homepage headlines and descriptions.
* Update the About Us section.
* Edit company information.
* Change banners and promotional images.
* Add announcements.
* Update contact details.
* Manage testimonials.
* Update social media links.
* Edit website text and images.

All published changes should reflect on the public website without requiring manual coding.

7. PROMOTIONS & SPECIAL OFFERS

Create a section where I can:

* Create promotional campaigns.
* Upload promotional banners.
* Add special offers.
* Set promotion start and end dates.
* Publish announcements.
* Feature selected promotions on the homepage.

Expired promotions should automatically stop displaying.

8. QUOTATION MANAGEMENT

Create a quotation system where I can:

* Prepare quotations for customers.
* Add requested services.
* Specify quantities and dimensions.
* Enter prices.
* Add delivery or production charges.
* Calculate totals.
* Generate professional quotation documents in PDF.
* Send quotations to customers through email or WhatsApp.

Allow customers to accept or decline quotations.

9. REVIEWS & TESTIMONIALS

I want to manage customer feedback.

Features should include:

* Add customer testimonials.
* Upload customer feedback images where authorised.
* Approve or reject submitted reviews.
* Feature selected testimonials on the homepage.
* Remove inappropriate or irrelevant reviews.

10. NOTIFICATIONS

The system should notify me when:

* A customer submits a new enquiry.
* A customer uploads a design.
* A new order is placed.
* A customer requests a quotation.
* A customer accepts a quotation.
* A customer submits a review.

Notifications should be available inside the admin dashboard, with optional email notifications.

11. WEBSITE ANALYTICS & REPORTS

Include an analytics section showing:

* Number of website visitors.
* Most visited pages.
* Most viewed services.
* Most popular portfolio projects.
* Number of enquiries received.
* Enquiry conversion statistics.
* Customer order statistics.

Allow me to view reports by date range and export useful business reports.

12. ADMINISTRATOR & STAFF MANAGEMENT

The website should support multiple authorised users.

I want:

* A secure administrator login.
* Ability to create additional staff accounts.
* Different access permissions.
* A super administrator account with full control.
* Staff accounts with restricted permissions.
* Activity logs showing important administrative actions.
* Secure password reset.
* Two-factor authentication where supported.

13. MEDIA LIBRARY

Create a central media library where I can manage:

* Logos.
* Project pictures.
* Service images.
* Promotional banners.
* Videos.
* Downloadable documents.

Include image previews, search, categories and file organisation.

Automatically optimise uploaded images for fast website loading while preserving good visual quality.

14. WEBSITE SECURITY & BACKUPS

The website must include:

* Secure administrator authentication.
* Protection against unauthorised access.
* Secure file uploads.
* Input validation and spam protection.
* HTTPS security.
* Regular database backups.
* Recovery options.
* Error monitoring.
* Secure handling of customer information.

15. FUTURE EXPANSION

Build the website so that additional features can be introduced without rebuilding the entire platform.

Future features should include:

* Customer accounts.
* Online payments.
* Customer order tracking.
* Automated invoices and receipts.
* Online store functionality.
* Product catalogues.
* Live chat.
* Delivery and dispatch management.

FINAL REQUIREMENT

I want a fully functional, dynamic website connected to a properly structured backend database.

The administrator should have complete control over the website’s content, portfolio, services, enquiries, quotations, customers, promotions and other business activities.

Whenever I upload a new project, add a service, update a picture or publish an announcement, the changes should automatically reflect on the public website.

I want a system that is easy to operate, secure, reliable, scalable and professionally developed.

The objective is to make EMMY-T CONCEPT a complete digital business platform, not just a company website.

In short 
EMMY-T CONCEPT specialises in Graphic Design, Digital Printing, Business & Corporate Printing, Clothing & Fabric Printing, Product & Packaging Branding, and Advertising & Promotional Solutions.
